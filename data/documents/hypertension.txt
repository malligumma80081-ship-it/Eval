Hypertension is a medical condition in which blood pressure stays higher than normal for long periods of time.
It often has no obvious symptoms, which is why it is sometimes called the silent killer.
Common symptoms can include headaches, dizziness, blurred vision, chest discomfort, and shortness of breath.
Hypertension increases the risk of heart disease, stroke, kidney damage, and other serious complications.
It is usually managed with lifestyle changes, medication, and regular monitoring by a healthcare professional.
